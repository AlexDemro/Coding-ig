Acoustic steel guitar samples — FluidR3 / MIDI.js Soundfonts

Six open-string notes (E2, A2, D3, G3, B3, E4) from the FluidR3 acoustic_guitar_steel instrument, pre-rendered by the MIDI.js Soundfonts project.
Fluid soundfont: Frank Wen and contributors. Pre-rendered soundfont repository: Benjamin Gleitz and contributors.

Source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
Sample source directory: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM/acoustic_guitar_steel-mp3

The bundled MP3 files are unchanged. FretMate trims leading silence, normalizes levels, calibrates the sample tuning, transposes to fretted pitches, and adds dynamics, filtering, stereo positioning, and strum timing during playback.
SHA256SUMS records checksums of the bundled files. No external sound service or API is required at runtime.
